# Archived: the "day clock" version (before v3)

This branch keeps the site as it stood on 2026-11-12 at `4ddf388`, the last
commit before the v3 redesign, so the two can be compared. Tag
`archive-2026-11-12-day-clock` points at that same commit.

What this version is: "A day in her life" carried by labels. Sections are
stamped with hours (07:30 to 22:30), a floating vlog timestamp follows the
reader, and her work sits on instant prints and a moodboard.

Why it was replaced: James's verdict was that it still read as AI-made,
with every section built from the same label, headline and block, rows of
cards and long lists. v3 lives on `main`.

Do not build on this branch. It is kept for comparison only.
