# Design directions

## Chosen: a day in her life (C, refined)

Her feed and James's brief pointed past all three. The look is C, Soft Studio, warmed to her feed and set in James's palette: cream `#FAF8F5`, blush `#E8D5CE`, sage `#C5D1C8` and espresso `#2C2A29`. The type is Cormorant (editorial serif) with Hanken Grotesk, plus Caveat in small doses for the hand-written notes her covers use.

The signature idea comes from her format, the day-in-the-life vlog. The site keeps time:

- **The page:** sections are filed by hour (07:30 cover, 08:00 Beauty & Hair, 12:30 F&B, 17:30 Fashion, the weekend's Travel, 15:30 the menu, 18:30 golden-hour About, 22:30 booking, 00:00 goodnight), and each is lit for its hour.
- **The clock:** a vlog timestamp in the corner follows the reader and opens the day's running order.
- **The prints:** her work sits on instant prints that develop as they arrive. "Everything" lays them all on a draggable moodboard.
- **The fish:** a small fish, from 妤🐟, is the one playful note.

The three phase-1 options follow, for the record.

# Three design directions

Phase 1 is neutral on purpose. One of these (or a better idea her content suggests) becomes the site once her material is in. The pick follows her feed: the colours she grades to, how she films, and what brands she attracts.

## A. Glossy: the cover girl

**Concept.** The site as an issue of a glossy beauty and fashion magazine. Each video is a cover, the brands are this issue's advertisers, and the rate card is the masthead.

- **Palette:** cream paper `#F6F0E8`, espresso ink `#2A1A14`, rouge `#B5183C`, blush `#F3C9C2`, and a gold-foil gradient used once or twice.
- **Type:** Bodoni Moda (a high-contrast Didone, optically sized) for cover lines; Inter Tight for text; small caps for kickers.
- **Motion signature:** cover lines that set themselves letter by letter, italic swashes that draw in, and page-turn transitions between sections.
- **Set piece:** the *cover stack*. Her videos are stacked as 9:16 covers with cover lines (brand, format, result). Scroll or drag flips to the next cover, the top one plays muted, and a tap opens it with sound.
- **Fits if** her feed is glam: beauty, makeup, fashion, polished light.

## B. For You: phone-native and playful

**Concept.** The site behaves like the apps she lives in, so brands see her content in its native frame: story bars, caption stickers and a For You feed.

- **Palette:** milk `#FFFDF9`, bubblegum `#FF8FB8`, butter `#FFE9A8`, sky `#BFE3FF`, ink `#1A1A1A`, with flat sticker shadows.
- **Type:** Bricolage Grotesque, chunky and characterful, for display; DM Sans for text; caption chips in the platforms' own caption style.
- **Motion signature:** springy sticker pops and wobbles on arrival, swipe-snap reels, a brand-name ticker, and taps that ripple.
- **Set piece:** the *story hero*. Her best five videos play as an Instagram-style story, with progress bars along the top, tap left or right to move, captions as stickers, and "swipe up" leading to the booking brief.
- **Fits if** her content is bright, trend-led and talking to camera: GRWM, try-ons, hauls.

## C. Soft Studio: a quiet-luxury moodboard

**Concept.** An airy, tactile moodboard of linen, film grain, polaroids and tape. It is calm and aspirational, and lets the content breathe.

- **Palette:** linen `#F7F3EE`, oat `#EFE8DF`, milk chocolate `#6B4F3F`, cocoa ink `#2E2420`, and one accent: sage `#A9B39B` or dusty rose `#D9B2A9`.
- **Type:** Cormorant Garamond, light, with flowing italics, for display; Hanken Grotesk for text; tiny tracked labels.
- **Motion signature:** stills that "develop" like polaroids (from white, through grain, to colour), slow parallax, and soft blur-in reveals.
- **Set piece:** the *moodboard*. Her stills and videos lie on a draggable canvas as polaroids and taped prints, clustered by content pillar. "Shuffle" re-lays them with a soft spring, and a tap on any print plays it.
- **Fits if** her feed is neutral-toned and lifestyle-led: cafés, home, basics, travel, wellness.
